System Design Matters
Domestic electrical systems should be designed around how your household actually uses electricity.

Good planning considers:

Usage

Daily routines and appliance demands.

Layout

Room arrangement and future renovations.

Expansion

Future upgrades such as EV charging or extensions.



Thoughtful design avoids overloaded circuits, inconvenient socket placement, and costly rework later.
Compliance
All domestic electrical work is carried out in line with current regulations and recognised industry standards.

This includes:

Compliance with BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations)

Testing, certification, and proper notification where required

Correct protection, isolation, and labelling



All work prioritises safety, long-term reliability, and clean professional installation standards.
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to upgrade my consumer unit?

 If your existing board is outdated or lacks modern protection such as RCDs, an upgrade may be recommended for safety and compliance.

Can you add sockets or lighting to existing rooms?

 Yes. Most upgrades and additions can be completed without major disruption, depending on access and layout.

Do you handle small electrical jobs?

 Absolutely. From minor repairs to full rewires, all domestic work is treated with the same level of care and professionalism.

Will I receive certification?

 Yes. All notifiable or regulated work is tested and certified where required.

How long does typical domestic work take?

 Smaller jobs may take a few hours, while larger installations or rewires vary depending on property size and complexity.

Can work be done during renovations?

 Yes. Coordination with builders and other trades helps ensure smooth progress during home improvement projects.
