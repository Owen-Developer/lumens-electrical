EV Charger Installation
EV charge points are installed for domestic and commercial properties, providing safe and efficient vehicle charging at home or work.

Installations include load assessments, circuit protection, cable routing, and full testing to ensure the system operates safely within the property’s electrical capacity.

Smart chargers allow scheduling, energy monitoring, and integration with renewable generation.

Frequently Asked Questions
Can any property have an EV charger installed?

 Most properties are suitable, though an electrical assessment is required to confirm capacity and installation requirements.

Can EV chargers work with solar panels?

 Yes. Many systems allow vehicles to charge using renewable energy generated on-site.

Are smart chargers worth it?

 Smart chargers provide scheduling, monitoring, and energy optimisation features that improve efficiency and reduce running costs.

Do EV chargers increase electricity bills?

 Charging does use electricity, but smart tariffs and renewable integration can significantly reduce costs.

Can multiple EV chargers be installed?

 Yes. Systems can be designed to support multiple vehicles with load balancing and energy management.

Will I receive certification after installation?

 Yes. All installations are fully tested and certified in accordance with current regulations.
