Commercial Installations
We deliver electrical installations for offices, retail units, warehouses, workshops, and other commercial premises.

Projects can be completed as part of new builds, refits, or ongoing business improvements.

Maintenance & Fault Finding
Unexpected electrical faults can interrupt business operations and impact productivity. We provide rapid fault diagnosis, repairs, and preventative maintenance to keep systems running smoothly.

Scheduled inspections and planned maintenance help reduce downtime, extend equipment lifespan, and ensure ongoing compliance with electrical safety regulations.

Compliance
All commercial electrical work is carried out in accordance with current regulations and recognised industry standards.

This includes:

Emergency lighting and workplace safety requirements

Testing, certification, and correct system protection
