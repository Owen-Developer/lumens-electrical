ABOUT US
Quality-Driven Electrical
Work With Zero Compromise
I’m Josh, founder of Lumens Electrical, an Eastbourne-based electrical company built on over a decade of hands-on experience and a genuine passion for the trade. I take pride in delivering work that not only meets regulations but exceeds expectations. Every project — from small domestic jobs to high-end lighting and full property renovations — is approached with precision, care, and attention to detail. For me, this isn’t just about wiring or installations — it’s about creating reliable, well-finished work that I can stand behind. The quality of my workmanship speaks for itself, and I believe in doing things properly, first time, every time. At Lumens Electrical, the focus is simple: honest communication, dependable service, and results that last.
