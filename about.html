ABOUT ME
Quality-Driven Electrical
Work With Zero Compromise
The business was built on a simple principle: electrical work should be done properly, thoughtfully, and with pride. After more than a decade in the trade, I saw how often jobs were rushed, corners were cut, and decisions were made purely on speed or price. But electrics are not just about getting power from A to B - they affect safety, comfort, lighting quality, and how a space actually feels to live or work in. That’s why Lumens Electrical takes a different approach. I run the business personally, which means every project is assessed and certified by the same person. Clients don’t deal with sales teams or subcontracted labour - they deal directly with me. This allows for clear communication, honest advice, and a consistently high standard of work. The focus is always quality over volume. I take time to understand how a space is used, how lighting should function and feel, and how installations can be future-proofed rather than patched together. Whether it’s a full rewire, a carefully planned lighting scheme, or an upgrade to an existing system, the aim is always the same: do it once, do it properly. Lumens Electrical is fully qualified and NAPIT approved, and all work is carried out to current regulations. Just as importantly, work is carried out with respect for your home or premises - clean, organised, and with minimal disruption. As the business continues to grow, the vision extends beyond standard electrical work into more considered, design-led projects and high-quality property improvements. The goal is to work with clients who value craftsmanship, transparency, and long-term thinking. If you’re looking for an electrician who takes pride in their work and stands by it, Lumens Electrical is here to help.
